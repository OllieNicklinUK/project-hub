02 What it is

Real presence, for real work.

Up to 100 people share a space at once — talking naturally with spatial audio, moving between rooms and buildings, working around the same 3D content as if they were in the same room. Start from ready-made templates or have environments built to match your brand, your site, or your imagination.

This isn't a video call with avatars. It's a persistent place your organisation owns: the training centre, the showroom, the campus that's always there when your people sign in.

03 Key uses

Training, meetings, events and education.

/ 01

Multi-user training

Run hands-on training inside shared, photoreal spaces — dangerous, distant or expensive scenarios made safely repeatable. Best for onboarding, certification and role-play at scale.

/ 02

Town halls & exhibitions

Bring a distributed audience together in one place for company events, exhibitions and recruitment — wherever they are, on whatever device they have.

/ 03

Product & site review

Walk a factory floor, a construction site or a full digital twin with remote stakeholders. Review the real thing before it ships, without flying anyone anywhere.

/ 04

Classrooms & education

Subject-specific learning spaces students can step into — bringing the complex, the costly and the impossible within reach of every classroom.
